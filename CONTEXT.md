# Share-it

A children's book sharing app between trusted circles (family, theatre group, school parents, workshops...).

## Language

**Library**:
A sharing circle: a group of people who agree to share a subset of the books they own with each other. It is not a physical place or a location-bound collection — it's a shared view over books owned by its members.
_Avoid_: Group, circle, community (the term used across the product is "Library")

**Book**:
A physical copy owned by a user. At any given time, it has a single real-world location (with its owner, or with whoever currently holds it on loan). The same book can be shared in zero, one, or several libraries at once.
_Avoid_: Copy, item
